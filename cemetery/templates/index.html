05 / WHY THIS EXISTS

Open source is made of people.

Every repo began with a problem, a curiosity, a late-night README, or a suspiciously ambitious TODO. The Software Cemetery is a small nostalgic FOSS archive that treats public code history as a story worth understanding—not an excuse to dunk on maintainers.

Inspired by old terminal screens, community archives, and the strange tenderness of finding a forgotten project that once mattered to somebody.
